About EF Tours

Transforming education through global experiences since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010 in Gurgaon, EF Tours began with a simple yet powerful vision: to make world-class educational travel accessible to students across India. What started as a small initiative to take local school students to historical sites has grown into a leading educational travel company serving over 500 schools nationwide.

Our founders, experienced educators and travel professionals, recognized that learning extends far beyond classroom walls. They believed that experiencing different cultures, visiting iconic landmarks, and engaging with global communities could fundamentally transform how students view the world and their place in it.

Today, we've successfully organized educational tours for over 10,000 students to 25+ countries, with a special focus on STEM education, cultural immersion, and leadership development programs.

Our Mission

To provide safe, enriching, and transformative educational travel experiences that broaden perspectives, enhance learning, and inspire students to become global citizens and future leaders.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted educational travel partner, recognized for our commitment to student safety, educational excellence, and creating life-changing experiences that shape tomorrow's global leaders.

Our Values

Safety, integrity, educational excellence, cultural respect, and sustainable tourism guide every decision we make in creating meaningful travel experiences for students.

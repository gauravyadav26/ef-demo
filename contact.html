Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our educational tours

What's included in the tour package?

Our packages include international flights, accommodation, meals, local transportation, entry fees to attractions, expert guides, travel insurance, and 24/7 support throughout the journey.

What are the safety measures for students?

We maintain a 1:8 adult-to-student ratio, provide comprehensive travel insurance, conduct background checks on all guides, offer 24/7 emergency support, and follow strict safety protocols at all destinations.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 6-8 months in advance for international tours to secure the best rates, proper visa processing time, and preferred travel dates. However, we can accommodate shorter notice bookings when possible.

What educational value do the tours provide?

Our tours are curriculum-aligned and designed to enhance classroom learning through real-world experiences. Students gain cultural awareness, language skills, and subject-specific knowledge while developing independence and confidence.

Are there payment plans available?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans with installment options. We also provide scholarships for deserving students and can work with schools to create customized payment schedules.

Can tours be customized for specific subjects?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating subject-specific tours focusing on STEM, history, arts, languages, or any other educational theme. Each tour is tailored to meet your school's specific learning objectives.
